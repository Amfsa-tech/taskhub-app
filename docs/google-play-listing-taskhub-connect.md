## Full description

Get everyday tasks done with Taskhub Connect, a local task marketplace built for people across Nigeria. Post a task, compare bids from nearby taskers, chat in the app, and pay through secure escrow. If you want to earn, find local jobs that match your skills, send bids, complete the work, and receive payment through your wallet.

HIRE HELP WITH CONFIDENCE

Create a task with the details, budget, date, and location. Receive bids from taskers, review profiles and ratings, compare offers, and choose the right person for the job.

FIND LOCAL JOBS AND EARN

Browse available tasks that match your services and location. Place a bid, agree on the work, keep track of progress, and build your reputation with reviews after completed tasks.

PAYMENTS DESIGNED FOR PEACE OF MIND

Task payments are held in escrow and released after the work is completed and confirmed. Taskers can track earnings, view transaction history, and withdraw available funds.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

• Post local services, campus tasks, errands, deliveries, and digital work
• Discover taskers by service, profile, rating, and location
• Chat directly before and during a task
• Receive task, bid, message, payment, and withdrawal updates
• Save taskers you may want to hire again
• Track active and completed tasks
• Review receipts, spending, earnings, and transaction history
• Report issues and contact support from the app

BUILT ON TRUST

Taskhub Connect supports identity verification, ratings, reviews, secure sessions, and in-app reporting. Choose taskers based on the information available in their profiles and keep task communication and payments inside the platform.

Whether you need help with an errand, a campus task, home services, or remote work, Taskhub Connect helps you connect with the right people and move the task forward.

Ready to get things done or earn with your skills? Download Taskhub Connect and join a growing community of clients and taskers in Nigeria.

## Release blockers to resolve before declarations or submission

1. The mobile app offers account deactivation, not account deletion. Google Play requires account deletion for apps that allow account creation.
2. The public deletion page says the mobile app has an instant Delete Account flow, but the current app has only Deactivate Account.
3. The deletion page and legal pages identify the app as TaskHub, while the requested store name is Taskhub Connect.
4. The privacy policy contains an unresolved minimum withdrawal placeholder.
5. The terms page contains bracketed commission wording that should be finalized.
6. No Play Store feature graphic or production screenshot set is present in this repository.
7. The binary display name is still TaskHub in `app.json`. Decide whether the installed app should also display Taskhub Connect before the production build.
